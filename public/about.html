A little less digital noise.

Mailbot is a Gmail and Google Drive cleanup tool. Sign in with your own Google account, inspect your messages or files, and confirm the items you want to move to Trash.

Gmail

Browse cleanup suggestions or index your mailbox by sender. Search and sort senders, review individual messages, and move selected messages to Trash. Cancel stops queued work; requests already submitted finish and are counted. Recent confirmed Gmail cleanups can be undone.

Google Drive

Browse files by type and ownership. Owned by me is enabled by default. Sort each type by size, name, or update date. Select files and review them before moving them to Trash. Google’s current permissions determine which files can be moved. Restore files through Google Drive Trash.

Connection and scanning

Each browser connects its own Google account. A valid connection survives a reload in the same tab; expired access needs reconnecting. This public web edition uses Google’s standard Gmail API, whose quota can make a first full sender index slow. Completed headers are saved locally and reused. The fast scanner in the local edition requires a separate local reader and is not part of this hosted website.

Data controls

Your Google data is processed in your browser and sent only to Google for API actions. Review Privacy & your data for storage, removal, and permission controls.
