A little less digital noise.

Mailbot is a Gmail and Google Drive cleanup tool. Sign in with your own Google account, inspect your messages or files, and confirm the items you want to move to Trash.

Gmail

Browse cleanup suggestions or index your mailbox by sender. Search and sort senders, review individual messages, and move selected messages to Trash. Cancel stops queued work; requests already submitted finish and are counted. Recent confirmed Gmail cleanups can be undone.

Google Drive

Browse files by type and ownership. Owned by me is enabled by default. Sort each type by size, name, or update date. Select files and review them before moving them to Trash. Google’s current permissions determine which files can be moved. Restore files through Google Drive Trash.

Connection and scanning

Each browser connects its own Google account. A valid connection survives a reload in the same tab; expired access needs reconnecting. Standard scanning uses Google’s Gmail API and saves completed headers locally for reuse. When a shared scanner is configured, Connect fast scanning enables bulk header reads using a secure, read-only connection to Gmail. The connection screen identifies the scanner before you authorize it. Bulk cleanup moves up to 1,000 emails per request, with three requests overlapping when quota allows.

Data controls

Standard cleanup and Drive requests go directly to Google. Optional fast scanning sends a temporary token and receives headers through the configured scanner without storing them on that server. Review Privacy & your data for storage, removal, and permission controls.
