A little less digital noise.

Mailbot is a Gmail and Google Drive cleanup tool. Sign in with your own Google account, inspect your messages or files, and confirm the items you want to move to Trash.

Gmail

Browse cleanup suggestions or index your mailbox by sender. Search and sort senders, review individual messages, and move selected messages to Trash. Cancel stops queued work; requests already submitted finish and are counted. Recent confirmed Gmail cleanups can be undone.

Google Drive

Browse files by type and ownership. Owned by me is enabled by default. Sort each type by size, name, or update date. Select files and review them before moving them to Trash. Google’s current permissions determine which files can be moved. Restore files through Google Drive Trash.

Connection and scanning

Each browser connects its own Google account. A valid connection survives a reload in the same tab; expired access needs reconnecting. Fast scanning is the only mail-scanning mode. Signing in connects the hosted reader for bulk header reads over a secure, read-only connection to Gmail. The app identifies the scanner before you authorize it. Saved browser headers speed up cached views; unavailable scans show a retry or reconnect message. Bulk cleanup moves up to 1,000 emails per request, with three requests overlapping when quota allows.

Availability and limits

Google currently caps the unverified app at 100 users. The free scanner can take about a minute to wake after 15 minutes without traffic and has monthly hosting limits. Up to eight scans run at once, at most two per account. Scans stop after 30 minutes and keep completed browser progress; retries currently reread the mailbox. No fixed email-count limit is imposed, and speed varies. Google access can expire and require reconnecting. Gmail quotas still apply to searches and cleanup.

Data controls

Gmail cleanup and Drive requests go directly to Google. Mail scanning sends a temporary token and receives headers through the configured scanner without storing them on that server. Review Privacy & your data for storage, removal, and permission controls.
