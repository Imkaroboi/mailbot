Privacy & your data

Updated October 15, 2026

Mailbot helps you review your own Gmail messages and Google Drive files, then move only the items you confirm to Trash.

Google data accessed

After you authorize an account, Mailbot reads Gmail message IDs, sender and subject headers, dates, labels, and size estimates. It reads Drive file IDs, names, types, dates, sizes, ownership, and the permissions needed to determine whether you can move a file to Trash. This web edition does not download email bodies or Drive file contents.

Where your data goes

Standard Gmail cleanup and Drive API requests go directly from your browser to Google over HTTPS. If you choose Connect fast scanning, the app shows the configured scanner hostname before you connect. Your temporary Google access token is sent over HTTPS to that scanner, which verifies your account with Google and opens a read-only TLS connection to Gmail. It reads message headers, labels and size estimates, then streams them to your browser. The scanner does not save tokens or mail to files or a database, or log their contents. They remain in memory only while your request runs. The hosting provider processes this connection and ordinary request metadata under its own privacy practices. The website host serves the application files and may receive ordinary website request information such as your IP address and browser details under its own privacy practices. Mailbot includes no advertising, analytics, or generative AI service.

Storage in your browser

Your temporary Google access token and account identity are saved in this tab’s session storage until they expire or you disconnect. Reloading the same tab can restore a still-valid connection after checking the Gmail account. Message headers are cached in browser IndexedDB, separated by account, to speed up later scans. Recent Gmail cleanup IDs and their original Inbox membership are stored in this tab to support Undo. Drive metadata stays in application memory.

Your choices and deletion

Disconnect by clicking the account button. This removes the saved connection; it does not erase previously cached headers. In Senders, use Clear saved index to delete that account’s header cache. Clear this website’s browser storage to remove all local account records, tokens, and Undo history. Revoke Mailbot’s Google permission at Google Account connections. Revoke and disconnect to end access; browser cache can be removed separately.

Trash and Undo

Cleanup changes only reviewed items after you confirm. Gmail cleanup supports Undo for recent confirmed operations. Drive files can be restored using Google Drive Trash. Google controls Trash retention. Mailbot does not offer a permanent-delete or Empty Trash action.

Sharing and Limited Use

Mailbot does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, or use it to train AI models. Mailbot’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. When you enable fast scanning, the operator’s configured scanner processes the minimum Google data described above to provide that feature. It does not retain that data.

Contact

For questions about this app, contact the operator using the developer support address displayed on Mailbot’s Google authorization screen. For Google account or file recovery issues, use Google’s own support and recovery tools.
